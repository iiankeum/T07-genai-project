# 「아차 모먼트」 짧은 공감 영상 — 이미지 먼저, 그다음 영상

> AI 초안 · 돌린 사람: 금*현 · Claude · 2026-11-23 · 검토 전
> 왜: 첫날 지표에서 8초짜리 「통화 끊고 아차…!」가 인스타 조회 1위(154), 유튜브 끝까지 보기 51.5%로 가장 잘 됐다. 같은 결의 8~12초 공감 영상을 더 만든다.

## 순서 (금*현 방식 · 2026-11-23)
1. **GPT 로 첫 장면 이미지**를 만든다(세로 9:16). 이때 카툰 1화의 인물 기준 그림 `../../toon/ep01-real/ref/boss-기준.jpg`(부품집 사장님) · `mech-기준.jpg`(정비소 실장님)을 **같이 올리고** 「이 인물로」를 붙인다 → 카툰 · 릴스가 같은 인물로 이어진다.
2. 플로우의 **이미지로 영상 만들기(Frames to Video)** 에 그 이미지를 시작 장면으로 넣고, 아래 「움직임」 프롬프트만 넣는다. 생김새 · 배경은 이미지가 정하므로 영상 프롬프트는 움직임과 소리만 적는다.
3. 플로우 영상은 하루 5개 무료 → 하루 4개 + 다시 뽑기 1개로 쓴다. 이미지는 GPT 에서 마음에 들 때까지 뽑아 두고, 영상은 고른 이미지로만 만든다.

## GPT 이미지 공통 꼬리 (모든 이미지 프롬프트 끝에 붙인다)
```
Vertical 9:16 photo (1080x1920), photorealistic, documentary editorial photography, 35mm, natural light, shallow depth of field, muted slate blue and cream tones with a touch of coral. Use the attached reference people exactly (same face, glasses, clothes). Plain clothes: no text, no logos, no name tags, no lanyards. No readable text, letters, numbers, signs, screens or license plates anywhere. Leave the top 20% and bottom 25% of the frame calm for captions. Fictional people, not resembling any real person.
```

## 플로우 움직임 공통 꼬리 (모든 움직임 프롬프트 끝에 붙인다)
```
Keep the person, clothes and background exactly as in the image. 8 seconds, handheld documentary camera, realistic motion. No text appears. No dialogue or speech; only ambient sound and small sound effects. No voices, no talking, no mumbling, no words in any language.
```

> A2 영상(11/23)에 영어 대사가 나왔다 → 「말소리 금지」를 더 세게 적었다. 그래도 말소리가 나오면 Claude가 원래 소리를 빼고 BGM만 쓴다.

## 1일차 (4개 + 다시 뽑기 1)

### A1 · 받아 적은 메모를 못 읽음 — 사장님
- GPT 이미지:
```
The auto parts store owner (reference) stands behind a small cluttered counter in the morning, one hand just putting a desk phone receiver down, the other hand holding a small paper memo close to his face, squinting at it. Shelves of cardboard part boxes behind him, softly blurred. Medium close-up, chest up.
```
- 플로우 움직임:
```
He finishes hanging up the phone, brings the memo closer, tilts it sideways, then turns it upside down, frowning. His face slowly freezes as he realizes he cannot read his own handwriting; eyes widen at the end. Slow push-in on his face. Sound: receiver click, paper rustle, a small confused breath.
```
- 얹을 자막: 「방금 받아 적은 거… 뭐라고 쓴 거지?」 → 「아차…!」

### A2 · 두 번째 전화가 첫 번째 주문을 지움 — 사장님
- GPT 이미지:
```
The auto parts store owner (reference) at his desk, holding a desk phone receiver to his ear with his shoulder while writing on a notepad. A second desk phone right next to him has its red light on. Morning light from a side window. Medium shot from slightly above.
```
- 플로우 움직임:
```
The second phone starts ringing loudly. He glances at it, hesitates, quickly hangs up the first call and grabs the second phone. A beat later he looks down at his half-written note with a blank, worried face. Sound: two different phone rings overlapping, pen scratching, a short sigh.
```
- 얹을 자막: 「앞 전화… 몇 개라고 했더라?」

### A5 · 퇴근하려는데 다시 울리는 전화 — 사장님
- GPT 이미지:
```
Evening in the small auto parts store, warm low sunlight through the window casting long shadows. The owner (reference) is putting on his jacket next to his desk, one hand reaching for the desk lamp switch. The desk phone is in the foreground, slightly out of focus.
```
- 플로우 움직임:
```
He clicks off the lamp and turns to leave. The desk phone in the foreground starts ringing. He stops, his shoulders drop, he slowly turns back toward the phone; the camera pushes in on his face as he suddenly remembers something he forgot. Sound: lamp click, sudden phone ring, footsteps stopping.
```
- 얹을 자막: 「퇴근하려는데…」 → 「아차, 그 주문!」

### A7 · 사장님은 「하나」, 실장님은 「둘」 — 두 사람
- GPT 이미지:
```
Inside the small auto parts store, the owner (reference 1) behind the counter and the mechanic (reference 2, coral cap, gray coverall) in front of it. A single small paper memo lies on the counter between them. Both lean over it. Wide medium shot, both faces visible.
```
- 플로우 움직임:
```
Both point at the memo at the same time, then look up at each other. The owner holds up one finger, the mechanic holds up two fingers. Each shakes his head. Quick, light comedic timing, small camera swing between their faces. Sound: store ambience, a short awkward pause, a phone ringing far in the background.
```
- 얹을 자막: 「하나라고 하셨잖아요」 「둘이라니까요」 → 「누가 맞지?」

## 해결 장면은 따로 뽑지 않는다
「아차가 있었다면」 장면은 이미 있는 데모 화면(주문서가 뜨는 장면 · Ask 아차 답변)을 붙여 대신한다 → 플로우 무료 몫을 아낀다.
